# Cutting Handout: 12-Week Contest Prep Macros

Prepared for club members by the coaching desk. Print and keep on the fridge.

## Baseline

Estimate maintenance at 32 kcal per kg of bodyweight for lifters training four
or more days a week, then take 15 to 20 percent off. Recalculate every three
weeks against actual scale trend, not against the calculator.

## Macro split

- Protein: 2.0 g/kg, non-negotiable, spread over three or four meals.
- Fat: 0.8 g/kg floor, for hormones and satiety.
- Carbohydrate: whatever calories remain, weighted towards the pre-training and
  post-training meals.

## Weekly checkpoints

Weigh daily, first thing, and use the seven-day average. If the average has not
moved in two weeks, cut 150 kcal from carbohydrate or add a session of easy
cardio. Never both at once.

## Refeeds

One higher-carbohydrate day per week at maintenance, carbohydrate up, fat down,
protein held. It is a training and adherence tool, not a reward, so schedule it
on the heaviest session of the week.

## When to stop

Stop the cut if sleep degrades for more than a week, if strength on the main
lifts falls more than 10 percent, or if menstrual cycles become irregular. Those
are signals to eat at maintenance for a fortnight and speak to a doctor or a
registered dietitian, not signals to push harder.
